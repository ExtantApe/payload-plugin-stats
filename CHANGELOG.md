# Changelog

## Unreleased

- **First version.** A Payload 3 plugin that counts users and shows the counts to the people the site allows. Totals; new accounts yesterday, in the last 7 days and in the last 30 days (whole UTC days); counts by each configured select, radio or relationship field, as a high-level table (any, none, roll-ups) and a row per value, including `hasMany` fields and fields inside arrays; and count bands, users with more than N documents in another collection.
- **Aggregate only.** Every read of the users collection is a count. Count bands are one grouped count inside MongoDB that returns a single row of totals. No response holds a name, an email or an ID.
- **Small-group hiding, by structure.** No count from 1 to 4 shows (default minimum 5). What is hidden with it is fixed by the layout of the report and never by the sizes of the numbers: a field's rows and roll-ups are hidden together, a field's "any" and "none" are hidden together with them, and a list of steps (new-account windows, count bands) is hidden from the first small step down. A hidden count says nothing about its size. A zero shows as "fewer than 5". A table whose counts disagree is hidden.
- **The total is public.** It always shows, and every other count is hidden against a reader who knows it.
- **`access` is a required function.** No default, and no fallback to the users collection's `access.admin`. The plugin refuses to start without it, or with anything that is not a function. Only an exact `true` allows.
- **Relationship fields need an opt-in.** A relationship is counted only when its collection is listed in `categories`. Auth collections are refused even when listed.
- **Fields with their own read access are refused** at start-up: a counted field, a band field, any field on the path to one, and the title field of a `categories` collection.
- **A dashboard card, an admin view and `GET /api/stats`**, read-only.
- **CI.** Typecheck, tests against a MongoDB service container, and build, on Node 22.

Tested: 126 tests against a real Payload 3.92.1 and a real MongoDB 8 in a throwaway container. Counts for every table on made-up data (40 accounts), including an account with two roles counted in both, the same value in two array rows counted once, deleted owners left out of bands, and UTC day edges. Hiding: each rule on its own and the expected hidden tables on the made-up site. A reader who knows the code: for six report shapes, every possible site up to 9 to 20 users (about 660,000 sites over minimums 2, 3 and 5), grouped by the page each produces, with no page ruling out a value from 1 to minimum - 1 for a small count beyond what the public total rules out. That check fails for four other engines: small counts hidden alone, a neighbor picked by size, "fewer than" labels kept inside hidden tables, and blocks left un-nested. A separate brute-force reader that only does arithmetic, on 3,300 random reports at minimums 3 and 5, with the total known. Refusals at the endpoint, the card and the view for logged-out requests, a user with no role, three other roles, a user who may open the admin panel and an admin-flagged user from another auth collection; custom access answers other than `true`; 32 start-up refusals for bad options, plus a missing `access` and eight values of `access` that are not functions. No name, email or ID in the JSON, the card or the view. Removal of the test database container on SIGINT, SIGTERM and SIGHUP, with a stand-in process in the suite and with real SIGINT and SIGTERM by hand.
Not tested: the card and the view in a browser inside a running Payload admin (they are rendered to HTML in tests, and the view's page frame from `@payloadcms/next` is typechecked only); the import map step; Postgres and SQLite; Node 22 locally (CI runs it); a site with real data; the exhaustive check on shapes larger than those six, such as a field with more than four values or three roll-ups; a reader who compares reports saved at different times, which the README states as a limit.
